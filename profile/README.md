## 🚀 Summary
Optical Flares for Windows is a video copilot optical flares after effects plugin that adds a dedicated light-effect designer to Adobe After Effects. You open its interactive editor, layer flare elements together, preview them in real time, and animate them so the light moves naturally with your footage. It exists to make convincing, camera-style glows and streaks quick to produce instead of assembling them layer by layer. Every flare you create renders natively as part of your After Effects composition.

## ✨ Feature Set
1. Build each light effect from stackable elements in a real-time interactive designer. 🎛️
2. Start from a large preset library and reshape any look to match your shot. 📚
3. Animate movement, flicker, and dynamic triggering for lifelike behavior. 🎬
4. Fine-tune color, brightness, scale, and position on every flare element. 🎨
5. Apply it as a native After Effects effect that fits your existing render workflow. 🧩

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open your After Effects project and pick the composition that needs light.
2. Apply Optical Flares to a solid or layer from the Effect menu.
3. Open the Options to launch the interactive designer.
4. Load a preset or build your own flare, then set its color, scale, and animation.
5. Check the motion in your timeline and render the finished composition.
